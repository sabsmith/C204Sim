SuitShop Growth Lab

A decision simulator & stress test for your recommendation report

How this works

  1. Build your strategy. Make the four core calls your report makes — growth engine, product and inventory, visibility, pricing.
  2. Run the simulation. See a three-year projection of what those choices produce, plus the client guardrails they protect or strain.
  3. Run the stress test. Six market shocks hit your strategy. Some you will shrug off. Some will hurt.
  4. Take the feedback. A consolidated list of the questions your client — and your professor — are most likely to ask, mapped to your report's sections.

Runs entirely in your browser: nothing you choose is submitted, saved to Canvas, or sent anywhere. Outcomes are illustrative scenarios grounded in the Fall 2026 case — not forecasts.

About this simulator (for instructors & the curious)

What it is. A deterministic, rules-based learning tool built for the C204 Fall 2026 SuitShop case. Students encode their recommendation as four strategic choices; a scoring engine projects six performance metrics, checks the client's three guardrails (affordable prices, lean inventory, a brand experience distinctly its own), detects synergies and conflicts between choices, and runs six market shocks drawn from the case's competitive landscape.

What it is not. It is not a forecast, a grade, or an AI judge. Every outcome traces to explicit rules, so two teams making the same choices get the same feedback — which makes it a reliable discussion prompt rather than a verdict.

Pedagogical intent. The feedback prompts map to the final report's required elements: the recommendation, the What/Why/How argument, implementation with risks and mitigation, and the pre/post SWOT. Students who run the simulator honestly should arrive at their team debrief with better questions, not answers.
